Winnow — brand directions

Three logo directions built from the existing signal-mark, aurora, and score-ring visual language.

01 — Signal Sieve

A five-bar waveform doubles as a subtle “W” and a sieve. Noise dots on the left resolve into one clean signal line on the right. Best for the horizontal lockup and the extension header.

02 — Aurora Wordmark

A seeded aurora mesh behind a crisp wordmark, with a small signal mark above it. Best for splash screens, onboarding, and the settings header.

03 — W Monogram

A bold “W” built from signal strokes inside a rounded app icon, shown at 32, 48, and 64 px. Best for the toolbar icon and favicon.
